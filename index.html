01 · Our Story

A little studio with a big love for beauty

Jagrat Salon began with one simple belief — everyone deserves to walk out feeling like the best version of themselves. What started as a passionate one-chair studio has grown into Vadodara's cosiest unisex salon, known for honest advice, gentle hands and results that speak for themselves.

From your everyday haircut to the biggest day of your life, we listen first and style second — and never let you leave without a smile.
